Sign in securely with Google to connect your account.

What you are authorizing

  • Your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture.
  • Read-only access to your Gmail mailbox.

Important: Gmail's read-only permission technically covers your mailbox, not just the Inbox. This application will display Inbox messages only.

By continuing with mailbox access enabled, you explicitly authorize the designated administrator to view your Inbox messages through this application. Your message contents are retrieved when opened and are not stored as complete email bodies in this application's database.

You can revoke this application's Google access through your Google Account settings.

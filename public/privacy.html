Privacy Policy

Loggy3 — Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Loggy3 collects, uses, stores and protects information when you connect your Google account to our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize Google sign-in, we may receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture.

If you separately grant Gmail read-only permission, we access Inbox information, including sender details, subject lines, message dates and message content when requested through the application.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate your account, display your connected account information, retrieve Inbox messages, provide message search and support authorized Inbox notifications.

4. Inbox Access and Consent

Gmail access is requested through Google's OAuth authorization process. The requested Gmail permission is read-only. When you connect your mailbox and expressly consent to the disclosed administrative access, an authorized Loggy3 administrator may view your Inbox through the service.

Loggy3 does not use this permission to send, delete or modify your Gmail messages.

5. Storage and Service Providers

Account information, authorization credentials and message metadata may be processed and stored on our application server and database infrastructure. OAuth credentials are encrypted before storage. Message content is retrieved when needed for display and is not intended to be permanently stored in our database.

We use third-party infrastructure providers, including Render for application hosting, Neon for database services and Google for account authentication and Gmail API access. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information is accessible to authorized personnel for the operation of the service and may be processed by the service providers described above. We may disclose information when legally required.

7. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain connected accounts and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements. You may request account and associated data deletion by contacting us.

8. Revoking Google Access

You can disconnect your account through the application where that feature is available, or revoke Loggy3's access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect account information and authorization credentials. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
